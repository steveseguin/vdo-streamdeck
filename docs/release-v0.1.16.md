This update fixes microphone release and guest dial controls. It includes the OBS microphone integration and improved icon sizing from v0.1.15.

- Changing a held microphone key's settings or switching profiles releases the original press. A later key release no longer turns the microphone back on.
- Queued volume and PTZ adjustments are discarded when their guest changes, reconnects, or is replaced in a numbered slot. New volume adjustments use the new guest's level.
- Dials recover after a settings read fails.

Download **ninja.vdo.streamdeck.streamDeckPlugin** below and double-click it to update. Your keys and settings are retained.

[Set up one button for your OBS microphone and English captions](https://github.com/steveseguin/vdo-streamdeck/blob/main/docs/captions-and-obs.md).
